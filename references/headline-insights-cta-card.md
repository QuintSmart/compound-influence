# Headline–Insights–CTA (deep card)

Operator card for message framing. Skills: `ci-hic`, `ci-frame`. Lighter top-down alternative to the Pyramid Principle (Minto).

## Why it matters

In the first ~60 seconds the buyer often decides not „buy?“ but **„worth more attention?“**. Bad sequence = bad analysis. Display (order, opening) can kill good packaging.

## Three parts

1. **Headline** — Take-home message. The one sentence that sticks on the walk down the corridor.  
2. **Insights** — Supporting arguments. **Rule of three:** working memory holds ~four chunks — three insights + headline hits the ceiling. Less noise, more signal.  
3. **CTA** — Specific next action. Action-form of the headline: the first move that sets things in motion. Names **what** and **by when**.

## CTA needs the most discipline

- CTA comes **last when speaking**, but **first when thinking**: *What must my counterpart do when this conversation ends?* Everything else builds around that.
- Verb depends on counterpart:  
  - Senior → approve / sign off  
  - Peer → own / champion  
  - Team → adopt / stop  
  - Mindset → reconsider / reflect  
- Litmus: Do they leave clear on the next step?

## When to lead with what

- Problem already clear → lead with recommendation / headline.  
- Problem still invisible → problem statement first (often after Pain/Cost/Gain), then Headline–Insights–CTA.

## Pyramid Principle vs H–I–CTA

| | Pyramid Principle | Headline–Insights–CTA |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Depth | Full argument architecture, MECE | One headline + max 3 insights + CTA |
| When | Large decks, formal recommendation | First 60 seconds, mail, live ask |

Both are top-down. H–I–CTA is the **lighter operating form**.

## Mini-example (pricing approval)

- **Headline:** Today we need your approval for nationwide rollout of the +$1 combo price increase; the 60-store test is strong — I'll walk the numbers.  
- **Insights:** Three reasons (First / Second / Third).  
- **CTA:** To hit Q3 launch, we need pricing approval by end of week.

## Daily practice

1. Start every draft with the key message: *If they remember one thing — which?*  
2. Cap at three supports; cut ~20% without losing meaning.  
3. Reorder for audience need, not work order.

## One-beat coaching

Default: lock CTA first, then headline, then at most three insights — confirm each beat. Batch only on request.
