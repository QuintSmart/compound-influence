# Authority trap & engagement signals (deep card)

Operator card for live buy-in and authority moments. Skills: `ci-live`, optionally `ci-default`.

## Authority trap

- Authority gets **compliance**, not **commitment**.
- Stop leading with title (big or small). If you have authority, assume people will not follow until you earn it. If you lack it, assume you can still lead.
- Do not trust a verbal yes. Look for engagement: follow-up questions, ownership of next steps, movement between meetings.
- Pleasant passive „yes… we'll see“ closes conversation — treat as soft no / deferral.

## Silence vs pushback

| Signal | Read | Move |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Pushback / challenge** | Often still in the conversation — engagement | Label, map fear/constraint, smaller ask |
| **Silence / closed answers** | Often checkout | Do not fill with features; reopen with label or smaller Open Ask; check Felt Problem |

## Compliance vs commitment

- **Compliance:** polite yes, no ownership, no follow-up.  
- **Commitment:** questions, challenges, next-step ownership, movement between meetings.

Do not celebrate compliance as a win.
