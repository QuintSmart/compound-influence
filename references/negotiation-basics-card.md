# Negotiation basics (thin card)

Thin operator card for live pricing / buy-in moments. Not a full Voss/GASP library.

## Listening as concession

- Listening is a move, not a pause. People often need to feel heard before they can move.
- Reflect what you heard before you counter. Cheap talk that skips listening escalates.
- Ask one clarifying question when the other side is heated; do not stack three.

## Labels

- Label the emotion or dynamic you observe ("It sounds like the risk feels one-sided").
- Labels invite correction and detail; accusations shut down.
- Prefer labels over early solutions when you do not yet know the Felt Problem.

## Compliance vs commitment

- Compliance: polite yes, no ownership, no follow-up, closed answers.
- Commitment: questions, challenges, next-step ownership, movement between meetings.
- Do not celebrate compliance as a win. Ask what would make this theirs to carry.

## Silence vs pushback

- **Pushback** — Still in the conversation; treat as engagement to map.
- **Silence** — Often checkout; reopen with a label or a smaller Open Ask, not a longer pitch.
- If you get silence after a price or ask, do not fill with more features — diagnose Felt Problem and Buyer Map first.
