# Pain / Cost / Gain (deep card)

Operator card for Felt-Problem coaching. Skills: `ci-pcg`, `ci-diagnose`, `ci-mirror`.

## Purpose

Before pitching, check whether the buyer **feels** the problem urgently enough to act. „Let's revisit next quarter“ often means: *not urgent enough for me right now*.

The buyer's calculation is never „Is this a good idea?“ It is: **Is this idea worth giving up what I already have?** Friction vs. value of saying yes.

## Structural rejection (sweetening fails)

1. Problem without the right buyer  
2. Buyer without a felt problem  

Discounts, freebies, softer payment terms do **not** fix structural rejection.

## Three axes

| Axis | Question | What you listen for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Pain** | How much does it hurt? | Do they feel it *now* (body/emotion/ops) or only abstractly? |
| **Cost** | What does it cost to keep ignoring it? | Cost of **inaction** (time, money, risk, status, exhaustion) — not only project cost |
| **Gain** | What's in it for me if I deal with it? | Personal gain: money, respect, relief, safety. Gain must be **felt**, not only calculated |

## Vitamin vs painkiller

- **Vitamin:** valued when life is fine — proactive, optimistic. Soft willingness; easy to defer.
- **Painkiller:** people often pay without negotiating because the pain is present.

If you only sell vitamins: find a buyer with painkiller pressure, **or** create awareness first (mirror) — do not push more evidence.

## Mirror when they do not feel it

People rarely realise they have a problem because someone presented slides. They realise when they **feel** it. Stop evidence → hold up a mirror → let them talk.

Starter prompts:

- *If I could wave a magic wand and give you exactly what you want — a year from now, what has changed?*
- *And right now — what's happening?*

Only when destination is clear does today's inaction become costly („another year of …“). Then solution.

## Coaching checklist

- [ ] Right buyer (budget / decision / resource) — else structural fail  
- [ ] Pain: felt vs abstract? Vitamin or painkiller?  
- [ ] Cost of inaction named in *their* world  
- [ ] Gain for *this person* felt (not only ROI slide)  
- [ ] Upside vs risk clear for this buyer  
- [ ] Invisible problem → mirror questions, no feature escalation  

## One-beat coaching

Default: one axis (or vitamin/painkiller, or mirror) per turn. Confirm, write Akte, stop. Batch only if the operator asks to fill the rest.
