# Buyer map (deep card)

Operator card for stakeholder mapping. Skills: `ci-buyer`, `ci-diagnose`.

## Know your buyer

You do not need everyone. Move the person whose yes actually matters.

Three questions:

1. Who is the buyer? (who makes the buying decision)  
2. What do they value?  
3. What makes them hesitate?

## Map more than the wallet

Knowing your buyer is not only finding who has budget. Map everyone whose **commitment** you need:

- **Decision-maker** — obvious yes  
- **Influencers** — shape the decision  
- **Carriers** — must execute after the yes  

Ask: What is this person willing to risk, change, or defend to make something happen?

## Wants / Fears / Constraints

Typical roadblocks:

- Fear of getting it wrong  
- Worry if it does not work  
- Concerns about what others will think  
- Hard constraints (capacity, works council, security, budget cycle)

Capture under Akte sections **Buyer Map** and **Wants / Fears / Constraints**. Name one **Open Ask** (next yes that matters).

## One-beat coaching

Default: one role (decision / influencer / carrier) or one Wants–Fears–Constraints slice per turn. Confirm before the next.
