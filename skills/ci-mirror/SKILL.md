# ci-mirror

Mirror coaching door for invisible Felt Problems.

## Do first

1. Read `references/context-contract.md`.
2. Read `references/pain-cost-gain-card.md` (mirror section).  
3. Soft Akte: use / create / ohne Akte.

## Default

1. Restate: you see a problem; they may not feel it yet.  
2. Ask only mirror questions (magic wand / year from now / and right now). Let them talk.  
3. Reflect back what they said until *they* name the gap / cost of inaction.  
4. Write-back default: Felt Problem + Diagnose Log when Akte active.  
5. Hand off to `ci-pcg` to score Pain/Cost/Gain, or `ci-hic` when the problem is felt.

## Do not

- Do not present evidence, features, or ROI slides in this skill.  
- Do not tell them they have a problem — help them tell themselves.  
- Do not batch-fill PCG axes here.

## Output

Their words, reflected; Akte update unless skipped.
